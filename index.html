Privacy Policy for Nicotine Tracker

Last Updated: August 23, 2026
Developer: BasicApps
Contact: basicapps.contact@gmail.com

1. Core Privacy Philosophy

BasicApps builds Nicotine Tracker with a strict privacy-first, offline architecture. We believe personal health, intake habits, and daily routines belong exclusively to you. The application requires no remote servers, no user accounts, and no internet access.

2. Zero Data Collection

We collect, transmit, store, and share zero personal data.

  • No Personal Identifiers: No name, email address, phone number, or credentials.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: No tracking SDKs (no Firebase, Google Analytics, Sentry, or third-party trackers).
  • No Advertising: Zero ads and no access to Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Diagnostics Sharing: Error reports and logs never leave your physical device.

3. On-Device Data Storage

All records—including logged nicotine doses, craving ratings, e-liquid recipes, and target limits—are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.

  • Local Isolation: Data remains private within the sandboxed application environment.
  • Backups: Exported JSON backup files are saved only to locations explicitly selected by you using Android's native Storage Access Framework.

4. Permissions

Nicotine Tracker requests no invasive permissions:

  • INTERNET: Not requested. The app is incapable of network communication.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Not requested.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Optional on Android 13+ solely for local day-rollover updates.

5. Data Deletion & Control

You maintain complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual logs or recipes inside the application.
  • Wipe all data at any time via Android System Settings (Settings → Apps → Nicotine Tracker → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

6. Age Requirement

Nicotine Tracker is intended for adults aged 18 and older seeking harm reduction or cessation tracking. We do not knowingly solicit information from minors.
